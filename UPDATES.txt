MIGRATION FROM V1.0 TO V2.0
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

✅ FULLY BACKWARD COMPATIBLE

   • Old expenses display (just missing category)
   • Stats work with partial data
   • No data loss during update

HOW TO UPDATE:
   1. Backup your current index.html (optional)
   2. Replace with new v2.0 index.html
   3. Keep manifest.json and sw.js
   4. Open in browser
   5. PIN: 6089 (unchanged)
   6. All your data is there!
